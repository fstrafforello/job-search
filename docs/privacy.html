Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Job Search Dashboard is a free, open-source desktop app for tracking job applications. It is made by Francesco Strafforello and runs entirely on your own computer. There is no account with us, no server of ours, and no analytics or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Your jobs, documents, chat history, profile (such as your resume) and settings are saved in a file on your computer, by default in %APPDATA%\Job Search Dashboard on Windows or ~/Library/Application Support/Job Search Dashboard on a Mac, or in a folder you choose.

The AI features run on your computer, either with the app's built-in AI or with LM Studio. What you write and your resume are processed there and are not sent to the internet.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks Google for these permissions only:

  • drive.appdata: to create and update one file (job-search-data.json) in the app's hidden folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • openid and email: to show which Google account you are signed in with.

The synced file contains the same data the app keeps on your computer (except settings that only concern that computer). It goes directly between your computer and Google. We never receive it.

Your Google sign-in is stored on your computer, protected for your user account (encrypted by Windows, or in your Mac's Keychain). You can sign out at any time in Settings, and choose to delete the copy in your Google Drive. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide sync to you. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Connections the app makes

  • GitHub, to check for updates (asks only for the latest version number) and to download updates and the built-in AI engine.
  • Hugging Face, to download the built-in AI model, once, if you choose the built-in AI.
  • Google, only if you turn on Google Drive sync.

None of these requests include your job search data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app's data folder (Settings → Open data folder) to remove your data from your computer. To remove the synced copy, sign out in Settings and choose "Sign out and delete", or remove the app's access in your Google account.
